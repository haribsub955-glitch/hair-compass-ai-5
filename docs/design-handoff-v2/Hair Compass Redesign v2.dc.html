Options with real evidence for pattern loss Based on your baseline: androgenetic, suspected. Change it in your baseline if that’s not right.
Minoxidil 5% topical STRONG
Visible regrowth in roughly 4 in 10 people at a year; works regardless of cause. Expect a temporary shed at weeks 2–6.
Confirm strength and frequency with a clinician.
Finasteride 1 mg STRONG
Slows the driver (DHT); the best evidence for holding the line long-term.
Prescription only — needs a clinician.
Discuss the sexual side-effect profile before starting.
Microneedling MODERATE
Boosts the response to topical minoxidil when combined; weaker alone.
Get depth and cadence guidance — overdoing it harms.
Ketoconazole 2% shampoo WEAK
Small studies only; a reasonable add-on for scalp health, not a driver of regrowth.
Worth mentioning at your next appointment, not a reason to book one.
