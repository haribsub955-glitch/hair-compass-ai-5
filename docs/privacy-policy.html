Hair Compass AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Hair Compass AI helps users track hair, scalp, routine, photo, lab, trigger, and procedure information for personal monitoring and clinician discussion support.

i

Private by design. Hair Compass AI stores everything on your device. Your records, photos, and Health data are not uploaded to any server of ours, and there is no account. One exception exists, and it is opt-in: if you turn on cloud AI answers, a limited tracking summary — never your name, contact details, or photos — is sent to an AI provider to write the answer you asked for. You are asked before the first request, and you can turn it off at any time.

Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, Hair Compass AI may collect or store:

  • Profile details you enter, such as hair goals, scalp sensitivity, and hair-loss context
  • Hair and scalp check-in entries
  • Routine, medication, supplement, procedure, lab, and trigger logs
  • Photos you choose to capture or import
  • Subscription and purchase status
  • Optional Health data, including sleep, mindful sessions, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, body mass, body mass index, and dietary protein

Health data

If you grant permission, Hair Compass AI reads selected HealthKit data from Apple Health. This data is used only to display health-related context inside the app and compare it with your tracking history. Hair Compass AI does not write health data back to Apple Health.

Photos and camera

If you grant permission, Hair Compass AI can use the camera and photo library to capture, import, store, and display standardized scalp and hair images for progress tracking and comparison.

AI features

Hair Compass AI's AI features are optional and are not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. They work in two ways:

  • On your device. Daily insights and label text recognition run entirely on your iPhone (Apple Intelligence and on-device text recognition, where supported). Nothing about them leaves your device.
  • Cloud AI answers (opt-in). The hair chat ("Ask Wren"), deep analysis, and ingredient summaries can be answered by a cloud AI model. This is off until you agree to it in the app. When on, each request sends a limited, structured summary of your tracking record — check-in values, dates, treatment names and adherence, lab values, logged notes, and health-derived values you chose to track (such as sleep or heart-rate variability) — plus your typed questions, and, for ingredient summaries, the text the app reads off the product label photo (the photo itself never leaves your device). It never includes your name, contact details, precise location, or photos; nothing sent is tied to an account, because there are no accounts.

Cloud AI requests are processed by DeepSeek (the AI model provider) to generate the answer. DeepSeek processes this content as a service provider under its own privacy policy, and its servers may be located outside your country, including in China. We do not use your requests for advertising, do not sell them, and do not build profiles from them. If you decline or turn cloud AI off, these features run on-device where your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, or simply stay off — the rest of the app is unaffected.

Subscriptions

Hair Compass AI offers optional premium subscriptions to unlock advanced features such as analysis and export tools. Purchase processing is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card information.

Affiliate links

Hair Compass AI may display affiliate links to non-medication physical products. If you open or purchase through these links, Hair Compass AI may earn a commission. These links are provided for convenience and are not medical recommendations.

How we use information

  • Provide tracking and logging features
  • Support progress review and comparisons
  • Generate optional AI-powered summaries when you request them — on your device, or through the opt-in cloud AI described above
  • Provide premium subscription functionality
  • Improve app reliability and user support

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and your photos and full records never leave your device. Information is shared in exactly three situations: with Apple to process an optional subscription through StoreKit; with DeepSeek to answer a cloud AI request, if — and only if — you have turned cloud AI on; and, if you tap an affiliate link, the standard web request your browser makes to open that link.

Your choices

  • Deny camera, photo, or Health permissions
  • Decline cloud AI when asked, or turn it off at any time in the app (You → Baseline → Cloud AI)
  • Avoid using AI-powered features entirely
  • Choose not to use affiliate links
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple

Medical disclaimer

Hair Compass AI is for tracking and educational purposes only. It does not diagnose disease and does not replace care from a licensed medical professional.
