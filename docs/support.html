Hair Compass AI

Support

Hair Compass AI keeps your hair, scalp, routine, photo, lab, trigger, and procedure history in one private place on your iPhone. Most questions below come down to one of two things: Apple manages your subscription, and your records and photos stay on your device — only the optional cloud AI answers send a limited tracking summary, and only after you say yes.

Subscriptions

  • Premium subscriptions are purchased through, and managed by, Apple.
  • If a purchase isn't showing up, use Restore Purchases inside the app.
  • To manage or cancel, open your Apple subscription settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

AI features

  • Ask Wren, deep analysis, and ingredient summaries are answered by a secure cloud AI model (DeepSeek) once you turn cloud AI on — they work on any iPhone that runs the app. The first time you open one, the app asks; you can change your answer any time in You → Baseline → Cloud AI.
  • Cloud AI requests send a limited tracking summary — numbers, dates, and notes you've logged — plus your typed questions and, for ingredient summaries, the text read off the label photo. Never your name, contact details, or photos. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent.
  • If you keep cloud AI off, these features run on-device where your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, on iOS 26).
  • Daily insights always run on your device.
  • These features are optional — every record works without them.
  • AI outputs are informational only and are not medical advice.

Health access

  • With your permission, the app can read selected Apple Health data (sleep, stress-related signals, and similar context).
  • You can change Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app or iPhone Settings.

Photos and camera

  • The app can capture and import photos for scalp and hair tracking.
  • Camera and photo permissions are managed in iPhone Settings.

Affiliate links

  • Hair Compass AI may include affiliate links to non-medication physical products.
  • These are optional and do not replace clinician guidance.

Guides

  • Balanced eating — how too little food and regularly unbalanced meals can both show up in energy, and how to build a steadier plate.

Medical disclaimer

Hair Compass AI is for tracking and educational use only. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
