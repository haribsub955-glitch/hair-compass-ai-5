Hair Compass AI

Support

Hair Compass AI helps you track hair, scalp, routine, photo, lab, trigger, and procedure history in one place.

Subscriptions

  • Premium subscriptions are managed by Apple.
  • Use Restore Purchases inside the app if needed.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple subscription settings.

AI Features

  • AI-powered features run on-device and require Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, on iOS 26).
  • These features are optional.
  • AI outputs are informational only and are not medical advice.

Health Access

  • Hair Compass AI can read selected Apple Health data if you grant permission.
  • You can manage Health permissions in Apple Health and iPhone Settings.

Photos And Camera

  • The app can capture and import photos for scalp and hair tracking.
  • You can manage camera and photo permissions in iPhone Settings.

Affiliate Links

  • Hair Compass AI may include affiliate links to non-medication physical products.
  • These are optional and do not replace clinician guidance.

Medical Disclaimer

Hair Compass AI is for tracking and educational use only. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
